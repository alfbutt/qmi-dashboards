⚙ Before the Call · 5 min
Prospect Setup & Offer Pick
Discovery is the same for all three offers. The pitch, price and close change with the offer you pick here (you can switch at any time in the top bar).
Prospect Info
Which offer? Match the prospect type

Start with the highest package they qualify for. Step down only when budget is the real objection.

Type A · Package 1
Hybrid: Agency + QJ Network
$10,000 upfront + ~$1,500/mo ads
  • Established builder or reno company, $50K+/month, or a developer adding renos
  • Burned by agencies, or weighing a franchise (Dream Renovations, Jim's)
  • Has the cash, wants certainty, "not driven by the price"
Type B · Package 2
Marketing Agency
$2,000 + GST/mo + $50–70/day ads
  • Steady small business, 1–10 staff, can afford ~$4K/month but not $10K upfront
  • On the tools, no time to call leads within 5 minutes
  • Wants their own brand, wary of lock-ins, wants to scale spend later
Type C · Package 3
Starter Package
$300 + GST/wk + $30/day ads
  • One-man band, start-up, or a trade adding a renovation division
  • Tight cash flow ("I can only spend about $1,000"), sees little value in agencies
  • Happy and able to call every lead within minutes
Never: sell the Starter to someone with no time to call leads, or the Hybrid to someone who can't afford it. A client on the wrong package won't get results and won't stay.

📞 01 · ~2 min
Set the Frame
Build rapport, establish authority, and agree how the call will run.
Opening Script
👋
"Hey [Name], can you see and hear me okay?"
No camera: "I can't see you, it's [time] here. You're not still in your pyjamas, are you?" (light, to get the camera on).
🏠
"You're the owner of [Business Name], right? How's the week been? Busy on site?"
🔨
"I'm [Your name] from Quality Jobs. We work only with renovation and building companies across Australia. Our founder Alfie is a chippy by trade and ran his own bathroom renovation company, so we know your world."
📋
"The first part of this call is just for me to understand your business: what you do, where your work comes from, and what you need to win more of the right jobs. Then we'll look at the gap between where you are and where you want to be. If it's a fit on both sides, I'll show you how we work and what the next steps look like. Does that sound fair?"
⏱
"We've set aside about 45 to 60 minutes. Are you still good for the next hour? And is there anyone else in the business who'd be part of a decision like this?"
If a partner is involved and not on the call, try to get them on now, or rebook. Don't present to half the decision.

🔍 02 · ~3 min
Find the Problem
Touch the surface of the pain they're going through. Don't solve yet.
Questions
❓
"Right now, what's the biggest challenge in growing the business, or what's not working as well as it could be?"
Listen for: inconsistent work (feast and famine), poor lead quality, tyre-kickers, too much time quoting, being undercut on price, relying on builders, no time to chase leads.
🔁
If they say "I just want more jobs": "Makes sense, you wouldn't be on the call if you were fully booked. Is it the number of enquiries, the quality, the consistency, or turning quotes into jobs?"
⏳
"How long has that been a problem?"
🪝 Dig, reveal the pain. Play their words back to them later.

📊 03 · ~5 min
Current State: Their Numbers
Hold your ground and get real numbers. They power the ROI maths and the cost of inaction.
If they haven't told you yet, ask
🏗
"Where is the company currently, then? Do you currently have work on? How many jobs have you got booked in? What's your pipeline looking like?"
🛁
"What type of work do you want more of? Bathrooms, kitchens, extensions, full renos?"
💲
"Roughly what would a typical [bathroom / kitchen / laundry] cost through your company? Would you like to increase the average project value?"
📐
"What margins are you typically working on?"
📅
"How many jobs can you comfortably take on a month?"
💰
"How much revenue are you turning over every month on average at the moment?" · If they don't know: "How much did you turn over last year?" · "Is that normal for you? How long have you been at that level?"
🧾
"How do you quote now? How long does a quote take you, and how many of your quotes turn into jobs?" ← find gaps in their quoting process
Typical: budget bathroom $25–28K · mid-range $27–35K · high-end $40K+ · kitchens $15–25K · extensions $125K+. Builder margins usually 20–30%. If their bathrooms come in at ~$19K, tell them they may be missing out on profit. We always market the bigger, higher-value jobs.
Their Numbers (feeds the ROI + cost of inaction)
If they've told you, replay it to create the gap
"So you've currently got [2 jobs on], they'll finish up in [3 weeks], you're working on smaller jobs you're not happy with, and you've got nothing booked in afterwards to keep your boys busy. Right?"
⏮ 04 · ~10 min · spend the most time here
Past Attempts: Create Doubt
We need to know exactly what they've tried, so our solution explains why everything else failed.
Questions
📍
"Where does most of your work come from now?"
📱
"Have you tried Hipages, ServiceSeeking, Oneflare, an agency, Google Ads, a franchise or network like Dream Renovations, or a business coach?"
💸
"What did you spend, what did you get, and what didn't you like?" · "Why don't you think that it worked?"
🤔
"Are you talking to any other agencies right now? Who, and what are they offering?"
Tap what they've tried → what to dig into + the gap to expose
If they ask about Google
"Google and Facebook both work. Google catches people already searching, so you show up when they're comparing prices against five other builders. It's expensive and transactional. Facebook and Instagram let homeowners see your work 10 to 15 times before they ever enquire, so by the time they call they already know and trust you. A renovation is an emotional, trust-based purchase, so we double down on Meta first and add Google later once it's working."
Assumptive questions: make them admit the gaps (ask 1–2)
You're looking for "no, we never did that."
🪞
Tyre-kickers: "What kind of ads were they running? Price offers, or video of your work?" · "What questions did they ask before booking someone into your calendar? Were they properly qualified?"
🪞
Not enough quotes: "How quickly were leads being called, and how many times before they gave up? What % actually picked up the phone?"
🪞
No numbers: "I assume they gave you a report on cost per lead, bookings and jobs. What did last month look like?"
The DIY question
🧰
"Have you ever tried running ads yourself? What's stopped you from doing all this yourself, running the ads and calling the leads in-house?"
Goal: get them to say out loud that figuring it out alone isn't an efficient use of their time, and it's more efficient to pay someone who already knows.

💸 05 · ~5 min
Cost of Inaction
Make them say why they need to act now. This prevents "I need to think about it" later.
Questions
📉
"If nothing changes, same leads, same income, how long can you keep going like this? What does that runway look like?"
🗓
"You said you're quiet for about [X] months a year. At [X] jobs a month and $[X] a job, that's about $[X] of work missed every year. What would that money do for you?"
🌱
"This is the best time to start. If you've got a sales problem now, you had a marketing problem 3 to 6 months ago. Starting now fills your calendar for [next season]."
Missed-revenue calculator
"You said you're quiet for about [X] months a year. At [X] jobs a month and $[X] a job, that's about $[X] of work missed every year. What would that money do for you?"
Thriving but wants more (the "greed" buyer)
"Your quoting is strong. The bottleneck is enquiry volume. At your close rate, 10 more qualified quotes a month is about $[X] in extra revenue. Every month you wait, that's what's left on the table."
Their reaction (exact words)

🌟 06 · ~5 min
Desired State
Set up the pitch. Make them picture the ideal situation. The personal reason is what they'll buy for.
Questions (go 3–5 layers deep)
🚀
"Let's fast-forward 12 months and say this is solved. What does the business look like? Revenue, jobs a month, team?"
👨‍👩‍👧
"Why that number? What does it let you do personally? More time with the family, off the tools, a holiday, hiring?"
🔎
"And why is that important to you?" · keep asking until you hit the personal reason
Common goals: consistency over size · 2–4 bathrooms a month · booked 6–12 months ahead · off the tools · better clients in chosen suburbs · their own brand to pass on · more sleep, less stress.
Their goal + personal "why"
