Frame the Call
Establish authority, set the agenda, and get them leaning in.

🔍 Phase 2 · ~3 min
Pain Discovery
Find the real problem. This is your ammunition for the entire call.
Objective: Get them to name the gap between where they are and where they want to be. Don't solve yet — just listen and take notes below.
Discovery Questions
❓
"What's the biggest challenge you're facing right now when it comes to getting new work in?"
❓
"Is it more a volume problem — not enough leads — or a quality problem — lots of enquiries but they never seem to convert?"
❓
"How long has this been a problem for you?"
❓
"On a scale of 1–10, how big of a problem is this for the business right now?"
Their Main Pain (capture it)
Tag the pain type: Lead Volume Lead Quality Inconsistency Low Margins Wasted Quoting No System

📊 Phase 3 · ~5 min
Current State
Map their numbers. This makes the cost of inaction real and personal.
Questions to Map Their World
💰
"So where's the business at right now revenue-wise? Roughly — ballpark is fine."
📈
"What type of jobs are you doing mostly — and what's your average project value?"
🏗
"How many quotes are you doing a week? And out of those, how many convert?"
🎯
"And if we look 12 months from today — what does the business look like if everything goes to plan?"
Their Numbers (capture)
🔑 Click when Desire Belief collected ✓
⏮ Phase 4 · ~10 min
Past Attempts
Exhaust every solution they've tried. This is how you position QJ as completely different.
Key mindset: You're not criticising their past decisions — you're agreeing with them that nothing has worked, and building the case that those options are fundamentally different from QJ.
Questions to Exhaust Past Solutions
🔁
"Before we go any further — what have you already tried to fix this problem?"
📱
"Have you tried HiPages or ServiceSeeking? What was that like?"
💻
"What about Google Ads — either yourself or through an agency?"
📣
"Facebook or Instagram ads? Any social media marketing?"
🤝
"Referrals, word of mouth — how much are you relying on that right now?"
💸
"And in total — if you added up everything you've spent trying to solve this — what do you think that comes to?"
What They've Tried (capture)
🔑 Click when Doubt Belief collected ✓
💡 Phase 5 · ~5 min
Make the Admission
Use assumptive questions so they conclude — in their own words — that nothing they've tried actually solved it.
The Socratic principle: People believe their own conclusions far more than your arguments. Ask the question — let them deliver the verdict.
Assumptive Questions
🪞
"So with [HiPages / Google / Facebook] — did they ever actually qualify the homeowner for you before putting them through? Or did you just get random enquiries?"
🪞
"Did they follow up consistently on your behalf, or was that all falling back on you?"
🪞
"Did they have a trained sales process built specifically for trades? Or did they just run generic ads?"
🪞
"So what you're telling me is — nobody has ever actually built you a full end-to-end system. Just pieces of it, right?"
Their Admission (their exact words)
🔑 Click when Solution Belief collected ✓ 🔑 Reinforce Doubt Belief ✓
💸 Phase 6 · ~5 min
Cost of Inaction
Make staying still more expensive than investing. Quantify the bleeding.
Questions to Make the Cost Real
📉
"So right now — if the lead problem stays exactly the same for the next 12 months — what does that cost you in lost revenue? Based on your average project value, let's figure this out together..."
⏱
"And how many hours a week are you personally spending chasing leads, quoting people who ghost you, or dealing with timewasters?"
😤
"What's that doing to you personally — the inconsistency, the stress of not knowing where the next job's coming from?"
The Money Line — say this
"Based on everything you've just told me — you're leaving somewhere around $[X] on the table every single month. That's not a marketing problem. That's a systems problem."
Their Response / Emotional Reaction
🔑 Click when Cost Belief collected ✓
🌟 Phase 7 · ~5 min
Desired State
Go deeper. Find what the revenue goal unlocks personally — the real "why behind the why."
Deeper Desire Questions
🎯
"You mentioned you want to hit [goal] in the next 12 months — what does that number actually unlock for you?"
👨‍👩‍👧
"Is it about the lifestyle? Time with family? Stepping off the tools? Getting out from doing everything yourself?"
🏡
"If the leads were flowing consistently and you had a real system — what changes first?"
💭
"What's been stopping you from getting there? Is it the leads, the sales process, or both?"
Their Personal "Why" (capture it)
Partner / Spouse — Confirm Support
👫
"Is your partner involved in the business at all? Or if you were to make a decision like this today, is that something you can do on your own?"

🏗 Phase 8 · ~10 min
The 5-Pillar Pitch
Summarise their story first, then present the system. They should be nodding before you finish.
The Transition — Bridge to Pitch
"So let me just recap what you've told me... Your biggest challenge is [their pain]. Your goal is [their goal]. You've tried [what they tried] and none of it stuck because it was never a complete system. And if nothing changes, you're looking at [$X] in lost revenue every month. Does that sum it up?"

Wait for them to say yes. Then: "Great — so let me show you exactly what we'd build for you."

Investment Presentation

"You've told me the problem, you've told me the goal, you've told me what hasn't worked. Everything you said points to the same answer. So the question is — are you ready to stop leaving money on the table, or do you want to keep trying to do this on your own?"
